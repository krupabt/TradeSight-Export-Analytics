### Recommendations from AI-Assisted Review

1. **Improve dashboard spacing:** Increase vertical whitespace and padding between KPI cards and chart containers on ultra-wide viewports to enhance executive scanability.
2. **Add KPI definitions and tooltips:** Embed interactive tooltip icons ($ℹ$) on executive cards displaying exact mathematical definitions and source column names.
3. **Improve chart labels and legends:** Increase font contrast and bottom margins on monthly longitudinal charts to prevent angle cutoff on smaller screens.
4. **Add filter quick presets:** Provide one-click filter buttons (e.g., *"Delayed Shipments Only"*, *"High Value > ₹1 Cr"*, *"Repeat Buyers Only"*) to expedite operational root-cause analysis.
